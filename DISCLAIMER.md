# Disclaimer

This repository is a statistical analysis of publicly available market data, public blockchain records and public venue documentation.
It is provided for research and informational purposes only.

* **Facts and interpretation.** Transaction amounts, addresses, timestamps and fee flows are drawn from public blockchains and can be checked with the code in this repository (the downloads need a free Envio HyperSync token). How wallets are grouped, how activity is described, and the conclusions drawn are the authors' interpretation of those data. Readers should check the data and reach their own view.

* **No finding of wrongdoing.** Terms such as wash trading are used in the sense of the trading literature, for trades that create volume without transferring risk. They describe patterns in data. Whether any transaction was unlawful depends on facts, including who owns the accounts and what the parties intended, that public data cannot show. Nothing here asserts that Limitless, its team, its affiliates, or any wallet holder broke any law, regulation or platform rule.

* **Attribution limits.** Wallet clustering is a heuristic. Every group is reported with the rule that produced it, alongside the one result that needs no clustering: the same address on both sides of a fill. Shared funding sources and shared multisig owners show a connection between addresses, not common ownership or control. This analysis does not identify who controls any wallet and makes no finding that Limitless or any person connected to it operated the wallets described. On-chain data cannot show off-chain arrangements, such as market-making agreements, that may explain the activity.

* **Third-party statements.** Statements by Limitless, its staff, critics, analysts and the press are quoted and linked so readers can see what was said. Quoting them does not mean the authors agree with them or endorse them either in part or in full.
