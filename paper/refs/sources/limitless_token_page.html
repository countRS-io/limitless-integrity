$LMTS is the core of the Limitless platform. It powers our rewards and incentive programs while aligning long-term value between traders, creators, and the community.

$LMTS is available on Aerodrome. Connect your wallet on Base, swap USDC for LMTS, and you're done.

You can earn LMTS through the points program (trading, referring other traders, and providing liquidity), by staking LMTS for rewards, and by promoting Limitless on X.

Staking locks your LMTS tokens to earn rewards. The current APY and staking details are shown above, or visit the Staking page to stake your tokens.

A portion of platform fees is directed to buybacks, staking rewards, and incentive pools, ensuring value circulates back to active users.
